Product Questions

  • Q: What is the difference between PV1-F and H1Z2Z2-K cables?
    A: PV1-F is TUV certified for European markets, while H1Z2Z2-K is dual-certified (TUV + UL) for both European and North American markets. Both use XLPO insulation for superior performance.
  • Q: What cable sizes do you offer?
    A: We offer 2.5mm², 4mm², 6mm², and 10mm² cross-sectional areas. 4mm² and 6mm² are the most popular for residential and commercial installations.
  • Q: Do you offer custom colors?
    A: Standard color is black. Custom colors (red, blue, orange) are available for bulk orders with additional lead time.

Certification & Quality

  • Q: What certifications do your solar cables have?
    A: All our cables are TUV Rheinland certified (IEC 62541), UL listed (UL 4703), and CE marked (EN 50618). Additional certifications available upon request.
  • Q: What warranty do you offer?
    A: We provide a 25-year performance warranty on all solar cables, guaranteeing no less than 90% power transmission after 25 years.

Pricing & Orders
