Country-Specific Requirements

South Africa

SANS 10359 (identical to IEC 62930) is mandatory. SABS marking required for grid-connected systems. TUV certification is often accepted as equivalent.

Nigeria

SONCAP certification is mandatory. Pre-shipment inspection required before customs clearance.

Kenya

KEBS requires a PCOC (Pre-Export Certificate of Conformity). TUV or IEC certified cables are generally accepted.

Saudi Arabia / UAE

ESMA (UAE) and SASO (Saudi Arabia) have specific requirements. TUV and CE certifications are widely accepted.
