Typical Applications

This DC cable connects battery racks, battery management systems and power conversion equipment inside energy storage systems. The low-smoke halogen-free construction limits smoke emission and corrosive gas in the event of fire, which matters for indoor cabinets and containerised storage.

  • Residential battery storage cabinets
  • Commercial and industrial BESS containers
  • Battery rack to PCS / inverter DC runs
  • Off-grid and hybrid solar storage systems
