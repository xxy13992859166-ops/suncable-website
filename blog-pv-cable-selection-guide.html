PV Cable Selection Guide 2024

Choosing the right solar cable is critical for the safety and efficiency of your PV system. This guide covers everything you need to know about PV1-F, H1ZZ2-K cables, and MC4 connectors.

PV1-F vs H1ZZ2-K: What's the Difference?

PV1-F is a dual-core cable (red + black) commonly used in European and Asian markets. H1ZZ2-K is a single-core black cable required for North American installations (UL Listed).

Key Certification Standards

  • TÜV — Required for EU market installations
  • UL — Required for US market (NEC Article 690)
  • CE — European conformity marking
