Solar Cable Procurement Planning: Budgeting for Your Project

How to plan and budget for solar cable procurement. Volume discounts, shipping costs, and total landed cost analysis.

Cable is ordered late and needed early

On most projects the cable package is released after the modules and the inverter are fixed, which puts it at the end of the procurement queue and the start of the installation sequence. That mismatch is where programme risk lives. Planning the cable from the construction schedule backwards, rather than from the bill of materials forwards, is the fix.

Build the budget from four blocks

BlockWhat it containsWhere it goes wrong
Cable by sizeMetres per cross-section, not a single blended figureBlended prices hide the size mix
Drums and packingDrum lengths, returnable or one-way, palletsReturn freight forgotten
Freight and insuranceTo the stated Incoterm and portCIF quoted, FOB specified
Testing and documentsBatch certificates, third-party inspectionAdded after award

Decide drum lengths with the electrician, not the buyer

Drum length is the single most under-planned variable. Too short and you add joints in the field; too long and you carry an offcut and a heavier drum around site. Get the run list before the order is placed and match drums to runs — this is the one part of the purchase where a supplier can genuinely save the customer money rather than shift margin.

Phase the delivery, not just the order

  • First delivery: the sizes needed for the first week of DC work, so installation starts on time.
  • Second delivery: the balance, timed to storage capacity on site.
  • Buffer: a small allowance of the most-used size for termination losses and site damage — cheaper than an air freight for 200 m.

Price risk belongs in the contract, not in the estimate

Copper moves and freight moves. A quotation that states its copper base date lets you see what changed if the order is placed weeks later; a quotation that does not leaves the adjustment to be discovered at invoice. Ask for the base date in writing, and agree in the purchase order what happens if it moves before shipment.

Plan the documentation with the delivery

Certificates, batch test records and packing lists should be scheduled as deliverables with dates, not requested at handover. If the consultant specified a document set, confirm it at order placement — amending a certificate after the drums are on site is the slowest link in any closeout.
