PV Cable vs Electrical Cable: What is the Difference?

Many buyers confuse PV solar cables with regular electrical cables, but they serve completely different purposes. Using the wrong cable can cause safety hazards and system failure.

Key Differences

FeaturePV Cable (IEC 62930)Electrical Cable (IEC 60228)
InsulationXLPO (Cross-linked Polyolefin)PVC or XLPE
Temperature Range-40°C to +120°C-5°C to +70°C
UV ResistanceYes (Weather Resistance 4)No
Double InsulationYesNo
Typical UseSolar PV array connectionsPower distribution

Can I Use Electrical Cable for Solar?

Absolutely not. Regular electrical cable will:

  • Crack and degrade under UV exposure within months
  • Fail at high temperatures (solar cables run hot in sunlight)
  • Not pass solar system inspections
  • Void your inverter and panel warranties

When in Doubt, Choose PV Cable

For any solar application—whether residential, commercial, or utility-scale—always use cables certified to IEC 62930 (PV1-F or H1Z2Z2-K). The small price premium is worth avoiding costly replacements and safety risks.
