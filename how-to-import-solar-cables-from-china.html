How to Import Solar Cables from China

Importing solar PV cables from China can save you 30-50% compared to local suppliers. This guide covers everything South African, Nigerian, Kenyan, and Middle Eastern buyers need to know.

Why Import from China?

  • Factory-direct pricing (30-50% below Western brands)
  • TUV, UL, CE certified products available
  • MOQ as low as 1km per specification
  • 7-15 day production lead time
  • Experience shipping to 50+ countries

Shipping from China

A 20ft container holds approximately 8-10 tons of cable. Typical transit times:

  • East Africa (Mombasa): 15-20 days
  • South Africa (Durban): 20-25 days
  • West Africa (Lagos): 25-30 days
  • Middle East (Jebel Ali): 18-22 days
  • India: 12-15 days

Avoid Common Mistakes

  1. Verify certification validity with local authorities
  2. Request samples before bulk orders
  3. Check SONCAP/PCOC requirements for your country
  4. Use reputable freight forwarders experienced with solar equipment
