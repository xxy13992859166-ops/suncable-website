Solar Cable Export Markets

Solar cable travels on a certificate, not on a price list. A PV1-F drum that clears customs in Rotterdam is a different document set from an H1Z2Z2-K drum entering Lagos or Jeddah. This page sets out which approval each region asks for, what paperwork ships with the cable, and how long it takes to arrive.

Where a market requires a mark we do not hold for a given construction, we say so before quoting rather than after the deposit. That is the whole point of asking for the destination port at enquiry stage.

Documentation that ships with the cable

Every order leaves with the certification package for the destination market: type test reports, the certificate itself, and the export documents the consignee needs to clear customs. If your broker asks for something outside that pack, tell us at order confirmation and we will confirm whether it can be added before the container is sealed.

Lead times

  • Standard production: 7–15 days from deposit, depending on size mix and drum lengths.
  • Urgent projects: express scheduling is possible on stock sizes — ask when you enquire, not after the PO.
  • Quotes: specification and price reply within 2 hours on business days.

Markets we serve

Buyers in more than 50 countries have taken delivery of our PV cable. Detailed pages exist for the markets where we have the most repeat business:

  • Kenya — KEBS import verification, PV1-F, MC4 and off-grid sets
  • Nigeria — SONCAP clearance, Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt delivery
  • South Africa — SABS, supply to EPCs and distributors
  • Saudi Arabia — SASO, utility and rooftop PV
  • United Arab Emirates — PV1-F, MC4, combiner boxes for Dubai and Abu Dhabi
  • India — PV1-F and H1Z2Z2-K for Indian EPCs
  • Australia — AS/NZS requirements on request

We also ship to the wider EU, West and East Africa, the Gulf, Southeast Asia, and Latin America. If your market is not listed, the answer is usually still yes — send the destination port and the certificate your inspector expects.

How to get an answer fast

Three things decide both the price and the paperwork: the construction (PV1-F or H1Z2Z2-K), the size mix in mm², and the destination country. Include those and the reply is a real quote, not a request for more information.
