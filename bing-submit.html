Bing Webmaster Tools

Submit your site to Bing for indexing and organic traffic from Microsoft search.

Step 1: Add Your Site to Bing

  1. Go to bing.com/webmasters
  2. Sign in with a Microsoft account (free)
  3. Click "Add a Site" and enter: https://xxy13992859166-ops.github.io/suncable-website/
  4. Click "Add"
Add to Bing Webmaster Tools

Step 2: Verify Ownership

  1. Bing will show verification options
  2. Recommended: Use Meta Tag method - add this to your HTML head: 
    <meta name="msvalidate.01" content="YOUR_BING_VERIFICATION_CODE" />
  3. Or use DNS TXT record verification (most reliable for GitHub Pages)
Verify Site

Step 3: Submit Sitemap

  1. After verification, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
  2. Click "Add Sitemap"
  3. Enter your sitemap URL: https://xxy13992859166-ops.github.io/suncable-website/sitemap.xml
  4. Click "Submit"
Submit Sitemap to Bing

Step 4: Request Indexing

  1. Go to "URL Inspection" in the left menu
  2. Enter each page URL and click "Request Indexing"
  3. Start with homepage: https://xxy13992859166-ops.github.io/suncable-website/
  4. Then submit key pages: products, blog posts, country pages
Bing SEO Tips: Bing gives more weight to exact-match keywords in titles and content than Google. Make sure your page titles include your target keywords like "solar cable manufacturer", "PV1-F cable supplier", etc. Bing also values social signals more heavily.
