How to Choose a Solar Cable Supplier

Complete buyer checklist for importing solar cables from China.

1. International Certifications

Always verify TUV, UL, CE certifications. Request actual test reports. Check if certifications are current and accepted in your country.

2. Factory Audit

A reliable supplier allows factory audits (virtual or in-person). Request a video tour of our Xi'an facility anytime.

3. Quality Control

Ask about QC process: incoming material inspection, in-process testing, final product testing, and packaging standards.

4. Lead Time & Stock

Standard cables ship within 7-15 days. Check if common sizes are kept in stock for faster delivery.

5. Payment Terms

Reputable suppliers offer T/T (30% deposit) and L/C at sight. Avoid suppliers demanding 100% upfront payment.

6. After-Sales Support

Good suppliers provide 25-year warranty, technical support, and responsive communication.
