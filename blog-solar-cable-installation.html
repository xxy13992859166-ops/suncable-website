Solar Cable Installation Best Practices

Proper cable installation ensures your solar PV system runs safely for 25+ years. Here are the key tips from our 20 years of manufacturing experience.

1. Cable Sizing

Always size cables based on current capacity and voltage drop. For string arrays, use 4mm² or 6mm² PV1-F cables. For larger commercial installations, consult our engineering team.

2. UV Protection

All our PV cables are Weather Resistance 4 (WR4) rated, meaning they can withstand direct sunlight exposure for 25+ years without degradation.

3. Connector Selection

Use genuine MC4-compatible connectors. Poor quality connectors are the #1 cause of PV system fires. Our MC4 connectors are TÜV certified with IP68 sealing.
