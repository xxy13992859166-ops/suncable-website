Solar Cable Price Per Meter - 2026 Update

Solar cable prices have dropped 15-25% in 2026 due to increased Chinese manufacturing capacity and lower copper prices. PV1-F dual-core cable ranges from $0.65/m (1mm²) to $2.80/m (6mm²) factory direct. H1Z2Z2-K single-core cable: $0.55/m (1.5mm²) to $2.20/m (6mm²). MC4 connector pairs: $1.20-2.80 in bulk. Prices vary by order quantity, certification requirements, and shipping destination.

PV1-F Cable Pricing by Size

1mm²: $0.65-0.85/m | 1.5mm²: $0.80-1.05/m | 2.5mm²: $1.10-1.45/m | 4mm²: $1.50-2.00/m | 6mm²: $2.20-2.80/m. Minimum order: 1,000m per size. Volume discounts: 5% for 5,000m+, 10% for 10,000m+, 15% for 20,000m+. FOB Shanghai pricing. Sample orders (100m) available at retail price.

H1Z2Z2-K Cable Pricing by Size

1.5mm²: $0.55-0.75/m | 2.5mm²: $0.85-1.15/m | 4mm²: $1.20-1.60/m | 6mm²: $1.80-2.20/m. Minimum order: 1,000m. H1Z2Z2-K is generally 10-15% cheaper than PV1-F of equivalent cross-section due to simpler single-core construction. However, MC4 connectors add $1.50-3.00 per pair to total cost.

MC4 Connector Pricing

MC4-Evo2 male+female pair: $1.20-2.00 in bulk (1,000+ pairs). Extension cable 300mm: $2.00-3.50. Male-female terminator pair: $1.50-2.50. Crimping tool (professional): $80-150 one-time cost. We supply genuine MC4-Evo2 compatible connectors that meet IEC 62852 standards at 40-60% below brand-name pricing.

Landed Cost by Region
