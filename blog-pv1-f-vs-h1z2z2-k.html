Overview: Two Standards, One Purpose

PV1-F and H1Z2Z2-K are the two most widely specified solar cables globally. Both serve the same function - connecting solar panels in arrays - but differ in geography, certification, and material composition. PV1-F follows IEC 62930 (Asian/American markets) while H1Z2Z2-K follows IEC 62541 (European/international markets). Understanding the differences helps you choose correctly for your project.

PV1-F: Dual-Core Convenience

PV1-F is a pre-assembled dual-core cable (red + black) with integrated connector channels. This eliminates field splicing, reducing installation time by 30-40% and labor costs significantly. Available in 1mm² to 6mm², rated 0.6/1kV AC and 1.8/3kV DC. XLPO insulation handles temperatures from -40°C to +120°C. TUV certified, UL listed for North American installations. Most popular in residential and commercial rooftop systems across Asia, Americas, and Africa.

H1Z2Z2-K: European Quality Standard

H1Z2Z2-K is a single-core black cable requiring separate MC4 connectors on each end. This provides flexibility for custom cable lengths and connector types. Also rated 1kV with 1500V DC capability. Z2Z2 jacket material offers superior UV, ozone, and weather resistance compared to standard PVC. TUV EL 23A certified - the gold standard for European utility-scale projects. Preferred for ground-mount solar farms and large commercial installations across Europe, Middle East, and Australia.

Performance Comparison

Both cables offer identical electrical performance: 1kV rated voltage, 1500V DC maximum for H1Z2Z2-K, excellent UV resistance (5 years tested), flame retardant properties, and 25-year design life. The key difference is installation approach - PV1-F saves labor time with its dual-core design, while H1Z2Z2-K offers flexibility for custom configurations. For most projects, the choice comes down to regional preference and installer familiarity.

Pricing Difference

PV1-F typically costs 10-20% more per meter than H1Z2Z2-K due to the pre-assembly process. However, when factoring in installation labor savings, the total project cost is often lower with PV1-F. H1Z2Z2-K requires additional MC4 connectors (~$1.50-3.00 per pair) and more labor time. For a 100kW system, PV1-F may save $2,000-4,000 in installation labor despite higher material costs.

Our Recommendation

Choose PV1-F for: residential/commercial rooftop projects, fast-track installations, markets requiring UL certification (USA/Canada), and when labor costs are high. Choose H1Z2Z2-K for: utility-scale ground-mount projects, European-standard specifications, custom cable lengths, and when connector type flexibility is needed.
