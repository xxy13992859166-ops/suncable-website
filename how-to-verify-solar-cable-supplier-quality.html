How to Verify Solar Cable Supplier Quality: 7 Critical Checks

Don't get scammed by fake TUV certificates or substandard products. This guide shows you exactly how to verify any solar cable supplier's legitimacy before placing your order.

7 Verification Steps

  1. Verify TUV/UL Certificate Online
  2. Request Factory Audit Report
  3. Check Production Capacity
  4. Order Samples First
  5. Verify Material Composition
  6. Check Export History
  7. Review Customer References

Check 1: Verify TUV/UL Certificate Online

Any legitimate supplier must provide valid certification. Visit tuv.com or ul.com and search the certificate number. If the certificate doesn't appear in the official database, it's fake.

What to verify: Certificate number, product scope (PV1-F / H1Z2Z2-K), standard references (IEC 62930, UL 4703), and expiration date.

Check 2: Request Factory Audit Report

Ask for a recent third-party factory audit report from SGS, BV, or TUV. A legitimate manufacturer will provide this within 24 hours. Watch out for suppliers who refuse or delay providing audit reports.

Red flag: Suppliers claiming "we don't do audits" or asking for payment before sharing audit documents.

Check 3: Check Production Capacity

Request photos or videos of your production line. A real manufacturer will have automated extrusion lines, quality testing equipment, and organized warehousing. Ask for specific details like daily output and number of production lines.

Check 4: Order Samples First

Never place a large order without testing samples. Order 100m of your required cable size and verify: print clarity, cable flexibility, insulation thickness uniformity, and copper conductivity. A quality supplier will ship samples within 3-5 days.

Check 5: Verify Material Composition

Quality solar cables use electrolytic copper (99.9% pure) and XLPO cross-linked polyolefin insulation. Ask for material test reports. Substandard suppliers may use recycled copper or PVC代替XLPO, which degrades under UV exposure.

Check 6: Check Export History

Ask for your export customs data or bill of lading copies (with customer info redacted). A legitimate supplier with real export experience will have documented shipments to multiple countries. Check if they have export history to your target market.

Check 7: Review Customer References

Request contact information for 2-3 recent customers in your region. Call or WhatsApp them directly. Ask about delivery time, product quality, and after-sales support. Legitimate suppliers will not hesitate to provide references.
