Typical Applications

This cable carries the AC side of a photovoltaic system — from the inverter output to the distribution board, and from there to the load. The three-core layout provides phase, neutral and protective earth in a single cable, which simplifies wiring on rooftop and ground-mount installations.

  • PV inverter AC output to distribution board
  • Rooftop and ground-mount solar installations
  • AC combiner and distribution circuits
  • Small commercial and residential solar systems
