Three agricultural loads, three different cables

Farm solar is not one application. A barn roof, an irrigation pump and a livestock building put the cable in three different environments, and a specification written for one of them is usually wrong for the other two. This page covers the building and pump side; field-scale agrivoltaics is covered separately.

Barn and shed roofs

Corrugated roof, dust, birds, and a structure that was never designed to carry cable. The practical issues are mechanical: fixings that do not pierce the sheet, cable kept off the roof surface so water can drain, and a route that a person can inspect without walking the panels. Where the run drops down a wall, protect it in conduit to the first disconnect — that is where machinery catches it.

Irrigation pumps and boreholes

Pump installations combine a long cable run with a wet environment and, often, a variable-frequency drive. Two points decide whether the installation behaves:

  • Voltage drop over the run to the pump. A borehole 200 m from the panel is a 400 m loop. Size on the loop and keep the drop inside the drive's input tolerance.
  • VFD harmonics and cable length. Long motor cables from a drive need the drive manufacturer's cable guidance — shielded construction and length limits are a drive requirement, not a PV cable question.

Livestock buildings

Ammonia, dust, rodents and washdown. Cable in poultry or dairy housing should be in conduit, fixed off ledges where dust collects, and specified with a halogen-free XLPO construction rather than PVC, which embrittles faster under chemical and thermal load. Keep connectors out of reach of animals and above washdown level.

One specification note that saves money

Do not use the array cable for the pump feed. They have different duties, different sizes and, in the case of a VFD, different screening requirements. Splitting them on the bill of materials makes the quotation comparable and the installation inspectable.

PV1-F 2-core and H1Z2Z2-K single core cover the array side, with the 450/750 V 3-core for the AC pump feed. Send the loads and run lengths to get a quotation.
